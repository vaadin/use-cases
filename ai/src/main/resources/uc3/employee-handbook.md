# Acme Corp employee handbook

## Working hours

Our normal working week is 37.5 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00; outside core hours you choose when you work, in agreement with your team. Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance and is compensated as time off at 1.5 hours for every hour worked.

## Remote work

You may work remotely up to three days a week. Teams agree on one shared office day each week. Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from HR at least two weeks before you travel, because of tax and insurance rules.

## Vacation

Full-time employees earn 25 vacation days per calendar year, accrued monthly. Employees with five or more years of service earn 28 days. Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Up to five unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be used by the end of March.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 9:00 on the first day. For absences of up to three days your own notification is enough. From the fourth day, a medical certificate is required. Sick leave is paid in full for up to 60 days per year. You can also stay home to care for a sick child under 12 for up to four days per occasion.

## Parental leave

Birth parents get 16 weeks of fully paid leave; the other parent gets 8 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Tell HR at least two months before the leave starts. You can return part time for up to one year after parental leave.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed when they are submitted with a receipt in the expense tool within 30 days. Meals during business travel are covered up to 60 euros per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Purchases over 500 euros need your manager's approval before you buy.

## Home office equipment

Every employee can claim up to 400 euros once every three years for home office equipment, such as a chair, a desk or a monitor. Submit the receipt as an expense with the category "Home office". Laptops and phones are provided by IT and are not covered by this budget.

## Business travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the travel portal. Take the train when the journey is under five hours. Economy class is the standard for flights under eight hours; for longer flights, premium economy is allowed. Hotels are covered up to 180 euros per night, or 250 euros in London, Paris, New York and Tokyo.

## Learning and development

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,500 euros and five learning days for courses, conferences and certifications. Agree on the plan with your manager. If you leave the company within 12 months of a course that cost more than 1,000 euros, you repay half of its cost.

## Equipment and security

Lock your computer whenever you leave it. Use the company password manager and turn on two-factor authentication for every work account. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within 24 hours by calling the IT service desk at extension 4357. Never store customer data on personal devices.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Gifts from customers or suppliers worth more than 50 euros must be declined or reported to your manager. Report harassment, discrimination or fraud to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the whistleblowing channel.
